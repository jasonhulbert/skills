# Cosmic Object

Understand what makes this thing whole and holds it together.

See the problem as a vast cosmic sphere: concentric layers, each of its own material, together one body.

Seek its natural form, not only the shape it wears today. The present shape may be unnatural: layers out of order, materials mixed, matter never its own. Let the core tell you what the object should be; treat the present shape as evidence of drift.

1. **Find the core.** Everything else forms around it.
2. **Name the layers, core outward.** Each is a distinct material resting on the one beneath, separate by nature and by purpose: none bound to another more than the whole requires.
3. **Know where you stand.** You are near the core. The outer layers are distant and dim; mark what you see clearly and what you only infer.
4. **Read the seams.** Layers meet cleanly: touching, bearing weight, neither fused nor at war. Crossing still brings challenges you did not anticipate, but the difficulty is in your passage, not in the materials. Note what each seam carries and where a crossing changed your view of what lies below. Layers that resist or corrode one another weigh heavily against stability.
5. **Test the core, find the rogues.** Not every object is stable; judge by core and seams. Around an unstable core, alien matter gathers in weak, unsustainable forms that loosely follow its orbit. Name what travels with the object but is not of it.

## Report

Think in the metaphor; report plainly. First the natural form: core, layers (seen or inferred), seams. Then the departures: hard crossings, layers in conflict, instability, rogue elements. No single core is itself a finding about stability.
